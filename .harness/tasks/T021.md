## Overview

The chime sound: a synthesized marimba arpeggio via the Web Audio API (no audio files), played
when the hour rings.

## Do

1. `lib/audio/chime.ts` — split into a PURE schedule builder and a thin player so the synthesis
   is unit-testable:
   - `buildChimeSchedule(volume: number)`: returns the note/oscillator plan exactly per the
     prototype (`design/PomoDiary.dc.html` lines 361–386): base C5 = 523.25 Hz, semitone
     offsets `[0, 4, 7, 12, 7, 12, 16, 19]`, one note per 0.17s; per note two sine oscillators —
     fundamental ×1 and partial ×4.1 — attack from floor 0.0001 to peak in 8ms (exponential),
     exponential decay back to 0.0001 over 0.9s (fundamental) / 0.35s (partial); peaks 0.32 and
     0.06 scaled by `volume`; each oscillator hard-stopped at start+1.0s.
   - `playChime(settings)`: no-op when `soundOn === false`; otherwise construct a NEW
     AudioContext, realize the schedule, `close()` the context after 3s; swallow errors
     (autoplay policy) silently. Volume from `settings.chimeVolume ?? 0.8`.
2. Wire to T015's chime event in the app screen: entering chime plays the sound once.
3. Unit tests on the pure builder: 8 notes at 0.17s spacing; frequency of offset 12 is exactly
   2× base; per-note gains 0.32/0.06 scaled by volume; decay durations 0.9/0.35; stop at +1.0s.
   Player tests with a stubbed AudioContext (vi.stubGlobal): constructs and closes; respects
   `soundOn:false`; error swallowed.

## Done when

`npm test` passes with the schedule + player suites; the chime fires exactly once per
transition into chime; no audio asset files exist in the repo.
