## Overview

The two full-panel overlays: the red chime screen ("TIME'S RIPE.") that any click acknowledges,
and the away screen (ASLEEP. / AT WORK.) with its live elapsed readout and return button.

## Do

Create `components/ChimeOverlay.tsx` + `components/AwayOverlay.tsx`, absolutely covering the
LEFT panel (`inset:0`). Reference: `design/README.md` "Overlays", prototype lines 139–159:

1. Chime (z-index 3, mode chime): the whole panel is ONE `<button>` filled `#ec3013`, white
   text, content bottom-aligned, padding 44/34/38, `wipeIn .4s cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1)`.
   Top-left at 40/34: a 30px solid white square with a second 30px square OUTLINE (3px border)
   over it running `ringPulse 1.5s ease-out infinite`. Headline `TIME’S<br>RIPE.` — typographic
   apostrophe U+2019, hard two-line break, `clamp(48px,7vw,78px)`/800/-0.04em/upper. A 3px
   white rule with `barGrow 1.4s`. Footer 13px/800/.16em/upper:
   `{fmtChimeRange(chimeFrom, chimeTo)} — click anywhere to account for it` (en dash inside
   the range, em dash after). Click → `acknowledge`.
2. Away (z-index 4, mode away): filled with the mode colour (`AWAY[kind].color`), white text,

   34ch). Bottom: a full-bleed white button (negative side margins per the prototype's
   `margin:0 -34px`), flush-left label `I'm awake` / `I'm back` with a 10px swatch in the mode
   colour, hover `#f3f2f2`. Click → `awayReturn`.
3. RTL tests: chime renders on mode chime and any click acknowledges; headline uses U+2019 and
