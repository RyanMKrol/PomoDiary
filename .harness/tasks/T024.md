## Overview

The zoomed-out grid: 10 days as rows by 24 hours as columns, with the hour ruler, per-day
counts, the category legend, and click-to-open-day.

## Do

Create `components/GridView.tsx` rendered by `Vine` when zoomed out, fed by
`GET /api/entries?rangeDays=10`. Reference: `design/README.md` "Grid view" + prototype lines
172–207 and renderVals lines 591–640:

1. Ruler row on grid `88px 1fr 42px` (14px gap): middle is `repeat(8,1fr)` labels
   `00 03 06 09 12 15 18 21` (zero-padded, 10px/800/.1em/.4 alpha), right cell literal `HRS`
   right-aligned.
2. Ten day rows (`i` 0..9, today first), same 3-column grid, 6px vertical padding, clickable
   (row click → set selected day + switch to day view), hover and selected-day background
   `#eae9e9`, `riseIn .4s`:
   - Left: `fmtGridDayLabel` (`Today`, `Yesterday`, `Sat 8`), 12px/800/.06em/upper, ellipsized.
   - Middle: `repeat(24,1fr)`, 2px gap, 22px-tall cells. A cell takes the colour of the entry
     whose START hour matches that column (first match wins when two entries share a start
     hour), else `#e6e3e3`. Each cell gets `title` from `gridCellTitle`/`gridCellEmptyTitle`
     (`9:00 · Comms — Inbox, mostly` — hour NOT zero-padded).
   - Right: the day's logged count zero-padded (`03`), 13px/800/.6 alpha, right-aligned.
3. Below: a 2px rule, then the legend — the 8 TAGS then Asleep and At work (10 items, NO
   Unfiled), 11px squares + labels (11/800/.08em/upper/.6) — then the line
   `Click a day to read the hours back.` (12px/600/.45).
4. RTL tests: 10 rows × 24 cells; a seeded entry colours its start-hour cell and sets its
   title; same-hour collision keeps the first; row click selects that day and returns to day
   view; legend lists exactly 10 items without Unfiled; counts zero-padded.

## Done when

`npm test` passes with the grid suite; the layout matches the measurements above and clicking
a row opens that day in the day view.
