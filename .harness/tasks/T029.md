## Overview

The hermetic browser-check harness (the house pattern from the owner's other repos): Playwright
driver scripts that build and serve the real app with every `/api/*` intercepted by in-process
fixtures and auth bypassed via a guarded env flag, then screenshot a manifest of states. Runs
locally and (later, T030) as the loop's visual-verification hook — never in CI.
