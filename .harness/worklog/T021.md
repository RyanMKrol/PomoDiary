## 2026-08-11

Built the chime sound: a synthesized marimba arpeggio via the Web Audio API.

- Added `lib/audio/chime.ts`, split per spec into `buildChimeSchedule(volume)` — a
  pure function returning the note/oscillator plan (8 notes at 0.17s spacing, base
  C5 = 523.25 Hz, semitone offsets `[0, 4, 7, 12, 7, 12, 16, 19]`, two oscillators
  per note — fundamental ×1 peak 0.32 decaying over 0.9s, partial ×4.1 peak 0.06
  decaying over 0.35s, both scaled by volume, 8ms exponential attack, stop at
  +1.0s) — and `playChime(settings)`, a thin player that realizes the schedule
  against a fresh `AudioContext`, closes it after 3s, no-ops when
  `settings.soundOn === false`, defaults volume to `settings.chimeVolume ?? 0.8`,
  and swallows any construction/autoplay-policy errors.
- Wired it into `lib/client/useTimer.ts`'s `notifyChime()` (the existing T015
  chime-transition hook), so the chime plays exactly once per transition into
  chime mode, sourcing `soundOn`/`chimeVolume` from the current settings. No app
  screen exists yet to wire into directly (`app/page.tsx` is still a placeholder
  and out of this task's scope), so the client layer is the correct wiring point.
