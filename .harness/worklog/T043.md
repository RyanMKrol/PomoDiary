### Two things learned empirically that the driver now works around (both local-only,
neither touches CI)

- `ClerkProvider` needs a syntactically valid publishable/secret key pair even when
  `E2E_BYPASS_AUTH` means Clerk itself is never consulted — the harness supplies fake ones
  (`FAKE_CLERK_ENV`). The fake key's encoded domain doesn't exist, so Clerk's client SDK
  fails to load its own JS bundle from it; those specific console errors (matched by host)
  are ignored, everything else still fails the run.
- On this machine, Chromium's own DNS resolution for `127.0.0.1` occasionally throws
  `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED` on the first attempt or two after a fresh launch (a VPN with a
  custom resolver running locally), even though Node's `fetch()` resolves it immediately
  every time. `gotoWithRetry` retries `page.goto`/reload a few times before giving up.
